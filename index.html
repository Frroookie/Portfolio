About me

I'm a Magna Cum Laude BSIT graduate from City of Malabon University (class of 2025). I got into computers young — curious about how websites worked, how things ran underneath what you could see. That curiosity turned into a degree, and the degree into building real things.

My flagship project is a full-stack inventory and POS system — role-based authentication, live cart management, stock tracking, and atomic checkout transactions with row-level locking. I built it to work correctly under edge cases, not just the happy path.

I also completed an IT support internship at Intellyx IT Solutions — Active Directory, network troubleshooting, hardware diagnostics. That experience taught me how software behaves in a real infrastructure environment, not just a local dev setup.

Outside of dev work: I genuinely like ducks. I had one growing up and I've been fascinated by the variety of species ever since — which eventually turned into Pondnotes.
